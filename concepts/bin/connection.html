Connection

After an abysmal divorce, Tori Bennett moves onto the next connection of her life: SoundCloud rapper Malcom. While his raps may not be great, he soars up the charts as their relationship unfolds.

Directed and written by Zachary Lindelof. Edited with Andrew Passantino. Starring Molly Berg and Christian Hockless. Cinematography by Skylar Anderson. Music by Edgar Rivera. First-Time Filmmaking Sessions finalist, 2019.
