Bearkat Sports Network

From 2017-2021, I worked for Bearkat Sports Network. As a full time film student, I worked for BSN as a technical director and video producer. This included filming/editing highlight videos, and producing live television broadcasts for ESPN3 and ESPN+. Below are select few broadcast I had a hand in directing and producing. Most of these productions were done on a Tricaster system or Ross video board.

Most games from SHSU can be seen on gobearkats.com or on ESPN+. Special shout out to Sheridon McGrew, Jason Barfield, Jake Vincent, Taylor Killebrew, Silas Banda, Darien Graham, and Brianna Padgett for being amazing crew to work with over the four years. Jordan Smith and Colton Foster also get shout outs as well!
