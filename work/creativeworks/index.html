2023

In 2022, I made a recap of my year. Inspired by feedback, I made a few creative recap videos of my year. Just as a way to keep my video editing skills intact- and practice a few new ideas, it has become a creative way to tell the story of my year.

All videos are filmed on a Sony HDR-CX405 camcorder to sell that home video effect. All of these recap videos can be seen on my Instagram, which you can see on this page.
